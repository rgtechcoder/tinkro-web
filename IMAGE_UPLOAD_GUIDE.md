## 📤 Imgur se Image Upload karne ke Steps:

### Step 1: Imgur par jaye
1. Browser me jaye: https://imgur.com
2. "New post" button click kare
3. Ya direct: https://imgur.com/upload

### Step 2: Image Upload kare
1. "Choose Photo/Video" click kare
2. Apne laptop se image select kare
3. Image upload hone ka wait kare

### Step 3: Direct Link Copy kare
1. Upload ke baad image par right click kare
2. "Copy image address" select kare
3. Ya image ke neeche "Direct Link" option se copy kare

### Step 4: Blog me Paste kare
1. Admin Dashboard me jaye
2. "Add New Blog" ya "Edit Blog" click kare
3. Image URL field me paste kare
4. Preview check kare

### ✅ Example URLs:
- Imgur: https://i.imgur.com/abc123.jpg
- GitHub: https://github.com/username/repo/raw/main/image.jpg
- Unsplash: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-xyz.jpg

### 🎯 Pro Tips:
- Image size: 500KB se kam rakhe
- Format: JPG, PNG, WebP best
- Dimension: 600x400 optimal
- Quality: Medium to High
